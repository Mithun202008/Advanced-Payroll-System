P373 [JUSTIFY (3) | sb=None, sa=76200, ls=1.25 | F=None, S=12.0, B=None, I=None, C=None]: Figure 1.1 summarises the end-to-end workflow. A transaction request arrives from a user, ATM or other banking channel and is authenticated. A session is established and the Banking Service prepares the transaction. The Behaviour Profile supplies historical context to the Fraud Engine, which executes every Fraud Rule. Each triggered rule returns a RiskContribution; the sum forms the total risk score, which the Decision Engine maps to one of four actions. In parallel, the Audit Logger, Alert Service and Event Monitor record and escalate the outcome, and the Administrator Dashboard presents it.
